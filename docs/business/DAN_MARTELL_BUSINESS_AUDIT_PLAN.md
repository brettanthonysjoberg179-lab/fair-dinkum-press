## 4. Tech Stack & Agent Architecture

Our technology stack is lean, modular, and purpose-built for maximum reliability:

- **Core Languages:** Python 3.13, JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML5/CSS3.
- **Backend & Webhooks:** FastAPI (`agentic_flow_agency.py`, `webhook_server.py`) running on persistent server processes and Cloudflare Workers.
- **Agent Orchestration & Integrations:** Composio API SDK connecting Gmail (`gmail_insane-fides`), Google Drive, Google Sheets/Docs, Stripe, Gumroad, GitHub, and Airtable.
- **Memory & State:** RAGS Vector Memory DB (`rags_memory_db`) for persistent lead profiling and business history.
- **Document & Asset Generation:** Custom Python ReportLab & Markdown-to-PDF / EPUB packaging pipelines.

## 5. Distribution & Sales Funnels (Gumroad, Stripe, Gmail, & Reddit)

We eliminated traditional sales teams by wiring our distribution directly into payment webhooks and API-driven outreach:

- **Stripe & Gumroad Storefronts:** Instant checkout links connected to real-time webhook listeners (`/webhook/stripe`). When a customer purchases, Stripe fires `checkout.session.completed`.
- **Automated Gmail Intake:** Our webhook server instantly captures the customer's email and dispatches a personalized welcome + 3-question intake questionnaire via the Gmail API.
- **Autonomous Fulfillment:** Once the intake is received, our audit agent generates the custom 15-page PDF report and emails it back to the client within 2 minutes.
- **Community & Content Syndication:** Automated Reddit posting agents (`publish_reddit.py`) and Telegram syndicators (`publish_telegram_teaser.py`) distribute value-first content and teasers across targeted communities.
