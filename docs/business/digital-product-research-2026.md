## High-Confidence Product Ideas

### 1. Australian AI Compliance Playbook for Small Business
- **Price:** $147–$197
- **Why it sells:** Dec 10, 2026 deadline — new APP 1.7 automated-decision rules under Privacy Act 1988. ~600K Australian businesses above $3M turnover threshold. The OAIC is actively enforcing — compliance sweeps started Jan 2026 targeting real estate, pharmacies, car dealers. Maximum civil penalty: $50M.
- **What's in it:** 90-day compliance checklist, privacy-policy template with AI disclosure section, internal AI-use policy template, decision tree for ChatGPT/Claude/Copilot use, Voluntary AI Safety Standard guardrails explained in plain English.
- **Workforce fit:** Research (Australian regs) → Draft (plain-English playbook) → Build (flipbook + PDF) → Package (cover + assets) → Publish → Sell via Stripe/Gumroad.
- **Demand evidence:** Multiple Australian sites (vtdigital.com.au, neomeric.com, aireriskaware.com, coterielabs.com.au) all writing about this — the gap between "general AI advice" and "AU-specific compliance" is wide and growing.
- **Australian angle:** The "Founder Tax" narrative ($92B AUD lost to admin) plus the Dec 2026 deadline creates urgent local demand + export potential for any business using AI globally.

### 2. AI Agent Workflow Playbook (Claude Code / n8n / Make)
- **Price:** $97–$147
- **Why it sells:** Market is flooded with "AI tools" wrappers; buyers want actual workflow blueprints. Gumroad already has successful examples at $19–$39 (underserved at premium). The 2026 shift is from "AI features" to "AI workflows that complete tasks."
- **What's in it:** 5–10 real agent workflows with copy-paste configs, failure data included (builds trust), multi-agent team design patterns, cost-tracking templates, guardrail setup for production runs.
- **Workforce fit:** Directly built from the workforce's own 25-agent, 3-LLM stack. Can document actual production workflows as the product. Authentic = sellable.
- **Demand evidence:** Agent Playbooks startup (apbks.com, 2026), Gumroad AI Agent Tools category growing, "5 Digital Products that Will Always Sell" lists include SOPs and checklists.
